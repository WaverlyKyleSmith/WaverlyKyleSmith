## Featured Projects
**[Trench Matchup Win Probability](https://github.com/WaverlyKyleSmith/trench-matchup-win-probability) Sequence vs. snapshot modeling of NFL pass-block outcomes from Big Data Bowl tracking data: an LSTM that reads every frame of a rep (AUC 0.813) outperforms an XGBoost model on sampled snapshots (0.757). Translated into blocker- and team-level win rate over expected (WROE), and the model infers on its own that tackles face harder matchups than guards or centers.<br>
[Dashboard walkthrough](https://github.com/WaverlyKyleSmith/trench-matchup-win-probability/blob/main/dashboard/walkthrough/README.md)<br>
`Python` · `PyTorch` · `PFF` · `Player Tracking` · `HTML`

**[Fourth Down Coaching Analysis](https://github.com/WaverlyKyleSmith/fourth-down-coaching-analysis)**
Win-probability-optimal decision modeling applied to 4th-down play-calling since 2018, paired with two custom coach-level indices (Decision Accuracy and Execution) finding a measurable leaguewide shift toward optimal play and a real, separable gap between reading situations well and converting on them.
[Interactive scatterplot](https://waverlykylesmith.github.io/fourth-down-coaching-analysis/visuals/scatterplot-all-coaches.html)<br>
`R` · `nflfastR` · `HTML`

**[Play-Calling Variation & Offensive Success](https://github.com/WaverlyKyleSmith/playcalling-variation-and-offensive-success)**
Drive-level analysis of whether unpredictable play-calling improves NFL offenses, using nflfastR + PFF data. Finds a concave (diminishing-returns) relationship between play variation and EPA per play. <br>
`R` · `nflfastR` · `PFF`

**[Rookie Contract Allocations and Team Stability in the NFL](https://github.com/WaverlyKyleSmith/nfl-rookie-contracts-and-team-stability)**
Team-fixed-effects regression testing whether rookie cap spending and roster construction relates to offensive volatility, finding head coach continuity as the strongest predictor. <br>
`R` · `nflfastR` · `SpoTrac`

**[Stolen Base Probability Model & Pregame Scouting Report](https://github.com/WaverlyKyleSmith/stolen-base-probability-model)**
Logistic regression on NECBL Trackman data (pitch velocity, movement, catcher metrics, count) to predict stolen base success, paired with a custom catcher metric ("Backstop+") and an automated PDF report generator for pregame scouting. <br>
`R` · `Python` · `Trackman`
